This preview is not signed with an Apple Developer ID. You do not need Terminal, and you should not disable macOS security.

  1. Try to open LegalCitationChecker.app once.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll to Security and choose Open Anyway.
  4. Authenticate if asked, then choose Open.

What to expect

Editorial assistance, with judgment preserved.

Start quickly

Quick Citation Audit checks a DOCX or PDF without requiring a saved project or source library.

Work locally

The desktop editions keep projects, manuscripts, and source-library data on the editor's computer.

Review honestly

Missing evidence produces a Manual check rather than an invented correction or overstated conclusion.

Important: Law Journal Workbench assists with the mechanical first pass. It does not replace Bluebook judgment, source review, substantive legal analysis, or an editor's final decision.
